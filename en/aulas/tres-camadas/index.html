Lesson 1 · 14 min

The company in 3 layers: you coordinate the agents, the agents serve the team

The business owner becomes the filter for everything: invoices, email access, CRM bonuses. To the person asking, it's urgent; to the business, it's rarely important. In this lesson you put a layer of agents between you and the team and write the contract for the first one.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Design the company in three layers
  2. 2Separate urgent from important with the Eisenhower matrix
  3. 3Write the agent's contract: it handles, it calls you, it never does
  4. 4Put a confirm button on everything that can't be undone

Goal: write the contract for the first agent, the one that takes the urgent-but-not-important requests off your plate.

  1. 1/4

    You

    You stop being the filter. You only get what's important: decisions, money above the limit, conflicts between people. Everything else arrives as a report.

  2. 2/4

    Agents

    Each agent has one area and a written contract. It handles what's on the list, calls you when something falls off the list, and never does anything irreversible without confirmation.

  3. 3/4

    Team

    The team asks through the agreed channel and gets an answer without waiting for the owner. Requests outside the channel are not handled: that is what keeps the layer standing.

  4. 4/4

    The three layers together

    The team asks the agent, the agent resolves or escalates, and you coordinate the agents instead of answering every request.

First order, then context and rules. Only then does the agent work well.

02 · From chaos to the first agent

Seven steps. Tap each one.

1. Inventory

Write down everything people ask you for during five workdays, one line per request, with who asked.

Don't solve anything differently: the goal is to see the real volume.

Inventory lineTuesday · finance · "where's the invoice for the email tool for September?"

2. Matrix

Sort each line into four groups: urgent and important, important but not urgent, urgent but not important, neither.

Urgent means a short deadline; important means it affects the business results.

SortingNew bonus in the CRM for tomorrow → urgent, not important.

3. Pick 3

Take three frequent requests from the "urgent, not important" group. They go to the first agent.

Prefer tasks with a known step-by-step: finding an invoice, setting up access, answering a process question.

4. Contract

Write what the agent resolves on its own, when it calls you, and what it never does. Use the template below.

A short contract works better than a long manual: one page is enough.

5. Channel

Set one fixed place for requests: a group, a chat bot, an email.

Tell the team the start date. A request that arrives another way is redirected to the channel.

6. Confirm

Purchases, payments, anything sent outside, and deletions always stop at a question: "Confirm?".

The agent prepares; you press the button.

7. Friday

Read the report: what was resolved, what was escalated, what went wrong.

Each error becomes a new line in the contract. That is how the agent gets better.

03 · Templates to copy

The contract goes in the agent's instructions. The message goes to the team.

Agent contract
AGENT: <name> · area: <finance | personal | support>

HANDLES ON ITS OWN:
- <task 1>  (e.g., find the invoices for this month's tools)
- <task 2>  (e.g., set up an email account for a new employee)
- <task 3>  (e.g., answer questions about the reimbursement process)

CALLS ME WHEN:
- money is involved above $ <amount>
- the request is not on the list above
- two people are in conflict

NEVER:
- pay, buy, delete or send anything outside the company until I click "Confirm"
- store a password or client data in plain text
- promise a deadline or a price on my behalf

CHANNEL: <where the team makes requests>
REPORT: every Friday, a list with resolved / escalated / error
Message to the team
Starting <Monday, month/day>, requests for <invoices, access and process questions>
will go to <channel>. The assistant <name> answers, right away.
If it doesn't know, it calls me. Requests on these topics sent any other way
will be redirected to the channel.

04 · Practice

Do this week. Move on to lesson 2 only when all three are true.

1 · Inventory on the matrix

5 days

Write down the requests that reach you over five days and sort each one into the four groups of the matrix.

Done when: the list has at least 15 requests, each with its matrix group next to it.

2 · Written contract

30 min

Copy the template and fill it in with the three requests you picked from the "urgent, not important" group.

Done when: the contract has 3 tasks under "handles on its own", 3 items under "never" and the limit amount in dollars.

3 · Team notified

10 min

Adapt the message and send it in the team channel.

Done when: the message went out with a start date, the topics and the channel.
