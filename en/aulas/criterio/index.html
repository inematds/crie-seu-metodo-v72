Lesson 7 · 15 min

Human judgment: the new-model test and your customer's sensitive data

If a new model comes out and your work disappears, you were working the tool. If a new model comes out and you just get faster, you were working your judgment. In this lesson you run that test, learn to separate sensitive data before plugging AI into your customer's information, and learn to prospect before you have social proof.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The new-model test
  2. 2Draft the plan before running anything delicate
  3. 3Three data groups: open, restricted, local
  4. 4Prospect with a case video and a landing page

Goal: apply the new-model test to your work and sort a customer's data into three groups before plugging in the AI.

01 · Your customer's data in three groups

Case: an architecture firm with all its files on a local server (NAS). Click each group.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

Give the client two options in writing: they own the data and are responsible for it, or they build it on your setup with clear rules.

02 · Judgment before tools

Four habits.

1. The test

Ask yourself: if a model comes out tomorrow that does what I do, does my work disappear or get faster?

If it disappears, you sell a tool. If it speeds up, you sell judgment and method.

2. Cooking

On a delicate topic (finance, sensitive data), spend hours on the plan before you execute.

Talk it through with an AI that has no context from the project, so you think from the outside, and only then build the plan with the agent that knows everything.

3. Separate

Before you connect the AI to the client's server, list the folders and mark each one: open, restricted or local.

Move the restricted data out of the folder the agent can see. Connecting first and separating later is too late.

4. Prospect

No testimonial yet? Record a short video explaining the case you handled and what changed for the client, without names.

Build a landing page with the video and a button for a 15-minute conversation. When you finish a project, ask for the testimonial.

04 · Practice

Close out the course with these three.

1 · New model test

15 min

Fill in the test with three of your own deliverables.

Done when: each deliverable is marked "disappears" or "I get faster" and you wrote what stays with you.

2 · A client's folders

30 min

Pick a client (or your own company) and sort the main folders into the three groups.

Done when: the table has at least 6 folders, each with a group and a reason.

3 · Prospecting with the case

40 min

Write the script for a video of up to 90 seconds about a case of yours: the problem, what you built, what changed.

Done when: the script has all three parts, no client names, and ends with the invitation to a 15-minute call.
